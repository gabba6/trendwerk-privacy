3. Was auf deinem Gerät bleibt

  • Deine Daten: Einträge, Ziele, Einstellungen, Notizen, Rezepte und Trainings liegen in einer Datenbank auf deinem Gerät. Darauf habe ich keinen Zugriff. Die App speichert nichts in iCloud.
  • Apple Health (optional): Wenn du die Verbindung einschaltest, fragt iOS dich nach Lesezugriff auf Gewicht, Körperfett, Schritte, aktive Energie, Ruheenergie, Trainings und Trainingsstrecken. Diese Testversion übernimmt davon Gewicht, Körperfett, Schritte und Energie; Trainings und Strecken liest sie noch nicht aus. Rohdaten aus Apple Health wie Herzfrequenz, Schlaf oder Routen verlassen dein Gerät nie.
  • Schreiben nach Apple Health (optional): Ein eigener, anfangs ausgeschalteter Schalter schreibt deine geloggte Ernährung nach Apple Health. Krafttrainings, die du in der App beendest, schreibt die App nach Apple Health, wenn du das Schreiben in iOS erlaubst; das kannst du in den Trainingseinstellungen unter „Workouts nach Apple Health“ ausschalten. Gewicht, Körperfett und Schritte schreibt die App nicht.
  • Daten aus Apple Health nutzt die App nur für ihre sichtbaren Funktionen, nicht für Werbung, Marketing oder Data-Mining, und gibt sie nicht weiter.
  • Kamera: Barcode- und Etikett-Scan werten das Kamerabild auf dem Gerät aus. Bilder werden nicht hochgeladen. Ein kurzes technisches Zeitprotokoll des Scanners bleibt auf dem Gerät.
  • Mitteilungen (z. B. Erinnerungen für Check-ins) plant dein Gerät selbst. Es gibt keine Push-Dienste.
  • Sicherungen und Exporte legt die App nur dort ab, wo du sie hinlegst. Wählst du für die tägliche Sicherung einen Ordner in iCloud Drive, liegt die Sicherung dort, auch mit Werten aus Apple Health. Was die App verlassen hat, kann sie nicht mehr löschen.

Klappt der Widerruf bei Apple nicht, kannst du die Verknüpfung in den iOS-Einstellungen unter deinem Namen › Anmelden mit Apple selbst entfernen.

2. What this is about

Trendwerk helps you log food, body weight, activity and training and calculates calorie and macro targets from them. The app is not a medical device and gives no medical advice.

The app works fully without an account and without a server. Then everything stays on your device and I receive no data from you. Account and server copy are optional. Without them you have no disadvantage, except that your data is not synced between devices.

3. What stays on your device

  • Your data: entries, goals, settings, notes, recipes and workouts are stored in a database on your device. I have no access to it. The app stores nothing in iCloud.
  • Apple Health (optional): when you turn on the connection, iOS asks you for read access to weight, body fat, steps, active energy, resting energy, workouts and workout routes. This test version uses weight, body fat, steps and energy; it does not read workouts and routes yet. Raw data from Apple Health such as heart rate, sleep or routes never leaves your device.
  • Writing to Apple Health (optional): a separate switch, off at first, writes your logged nutrition to Apple Health. Strength workouts you finish in the app are written to Apple Health if you allow writing in iOS; you can turn this off in the training settings under “Workouts to Apple Health”. The app does not write weight, body fat or steps.
  • The app uses data from Apple Health only for its visible features, not for advertising, marketing or data mining, and does not pass it on.
  • Camera: barcode and label scanning analyse the camera image on the device. Images are not uploaded. A short technical timing log of the scanner stays on the device.
  • Notifications (for example check-in reminders) are scheduled by your device itself. There are no push services.
  • Backups and exports are saved only where you put them. If you pick an iCloud Drive folder for the daily backup, the backup is stored there, including values from Apple Health. Once a file has left the app, the app can no longer delete it.

4. Account: Sign in with Apple

For the server copy you sign in with “Sign in with Apple”. The app asks only for the email address, not for your name. Apple gives me an identifier valid only for this app and, as you choose, your email address or an Apple relay address. For your account the server stores this information plus the times of creation, sign-in and sign-in sessions. I use it only to run your account.

So that Sign in with Apple can be revoked when you delete your account, the app passes a one-time code from Apple to the server at sign-in. The server exchanges it with Apple for a key and stores the key encrypted. When you delete your account, the server revokes the sign-in at Apple and deletes the key. If the revocation at Apple fails, you can remove the link yourself in iOS Settings under your name › Sign in with Apple.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (taking part in the test version). Apple processes the sign-in itself under its own responsibility (Apple Privacy Policy).

5. Server copy: what is uploaded with your consent

Only if you expressly consent, and for each data type on its own, does the app upload a copy of that data to the server, so that you can use several devices and have a copy outside your device. All data types are off at first. You give consent in the app under “Sync & Server”.

One device, one account: the app remembers the first account that signs in on a device. If a different account signs in there, sync stays off so that data of different people is not mixed.

Automated decisions: there is no solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effect (Art. 22 GDPR). Calorie and macro targets are suggestions the app calculates on your device, and you can change them.

8. Google Health (optional)

If you expressly turn on the connection, you sign in with Google in a browser window. Google is the recipient of the sign-in. My server receives an access key and stores it encrypted in Frankfurt, together with the time of connection, the last fetch, the permissions you granted and the version of the consent text. During sign-in an encrypted sign-in state is kept on the server for at most 10 minutes.

With the key the server fetches, read-only and for you, the readings of the last 14 days from Google: daily steps, active energy and total calories and, if you also grant Google the permission for body measurements, weight and body fat. The server passes the values on to your device and does not store them. In the test version they stay on your device and never go into the server copy.

The app is in Google's test mode: only registered test users can connect (for this I add your Gmail address to my Google project), Google shows a “not verified” notice and ends the connection after 7 days; then you tap “Reconnect Google”.

Disconnecting: in the app (the key is deleted on the server and revoked at Google) or in your Google account. Deleting your account does this automatically.

Legal basis: your explicit consent, Art. 9(2)(a) together with Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Limited Use: Trendwerk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Google Health API Developer and User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. I use this data only for the visible feature of the app, do not pass it on to third parties (including AI services), do not use it for advertising and do not let humans read it, except with your consent, for security reasons or when the law requires it.

11. Retention and deletion

  • Server copy: stays as long as your account exists and the consent for that type applies.
  • Withdrawing a data type: the server copy is deleted right away; the sync copy at PowerSync is removed at the next daily compaction, usually within 24 hours.
  • Delete account (in the app under “Sync & Server” › “Delete account”): deletes your account, all server data, the consent records, the Google connection (with revocation at Google) and the Apple key (with revocation of the sign-in at Apple). Data on your device stays.
  • Server backups do not exist. So no backup copies remain with the providers after deletion. The server is not a backup either: keep your own backups or exports.
  • Inactive accounts: accounts that have neither signed in nor synced for 6 months are deleted automatically by the server, the same way as “Delete account”.
  • End of the test version: the test version runs until 30 June 2027. 30 days after that the server automatically deletes all accounts and server data.
  • “Erase All Data” in the app removes the data on the device. The server copy stays until you delete your account or withdraw consents.
  • Logs: see section 7. Emails with requests I delete once the request is done, at the latest 30 days after the test version ends, unless the law requires keeping them.

14. Security

Transfer is encrypted (TLS). The providers store data encrypted (AES-256). Access keys for Google and Apple are additionally encrypted one by one on the server. Access rules in the database (Row Level Security) make sure each account can read and write only its own data, and the server checks again on every upload that your consent is in place. Server keys are not in the app. In a data breach I inform the supervisory authority and, if there is a high risk for you, you (Art. 33 and 34 GDPR).
